STOP–CHECK–VERIFY–REPORT

Use this quick reference at the moment of need.

1

STOP

Do not click, reply, download, or enter credentials while the request is unverified.

2

CHECK

Inspect sender address, link destination, urgency, spelling, context, and whether the request was expected.

3

VERIFY

Use a known phone number, official app, or separate trusted channel—not the contact information inside the suspicious message.

4

REPORT

Use the organization’s approved reporting process so the security team can investigate.

Remember: When risk or authority is unclear, pause and use the approved escalation path.
