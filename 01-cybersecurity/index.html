Case Study

Phishing Defense

A scenario-based cybersecurity experience designed to change employee behavior at the moment a suspicious message arrives.

Launch Interactive Course Storyboard Design Document

Business problem

Problem

Employees may recognize the word “phishing” but still act on urgent, credible-looking requests. The gap is not awareness alone; it is decision-making under pressure.

Audience

Office and remote employees who use email, chat, cloud applications, and company credentials.

Solution

Identify at least four warning signs in a suspicious email or message and choose the safest response.

Learning objectives

  1. Identify at least four warning signs in a suspicious email or message.
  2. Choose the safest response to a suspected phishing attempt.
  3. Apply a simple verification process before clicking links or sharing information.

Deliverables

Needs Analysis

Defines the behavior gap, audience, constraints, and measures.

Interactive Course

Three realistic message-analysis scenarios with feedback and a scored final challenge.

Storyboard

Screen-level content, interactions, feedback, and rationale.

Job Aid

A reusable STOP–CHECK–VERIFY–REPORT decision guide.

Evaluation plan

KPI

80%+ target final assessment score.

KPI

Improved identification of phishing warning signs.

KPI

Safer verification and reporting behavior.

Design rationale

The solution emphasizes decisions and job behaviors rather than information exposure. Practice is aligned directly to the objectives.
