Process

How I design

01 Analyze

Clarify the performance problem, audience, environment, constraints, and success measures.

02 Design

Write measurable objectives, map practice to job behaviors, and select the smallest effective learning solution.

03 Develop

Create storyboards, scenarios, assessments, performance support, and interactive prototypes.

04 Evaluate

Measure learning and transfer using completion, assessment, confidence, and workplace performance indicators.
