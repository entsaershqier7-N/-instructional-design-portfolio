My Design Approach

I begin by identifying the performance problem, target audience, business need, and desired learner behavior. I then develop measurable learning objectives and select the instructional strategy that best supports performance on the job. My projects use realistic practice, decisions, feedback, and performance support rather than relying only on information presentation.
