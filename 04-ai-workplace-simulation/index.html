AI at Work: Make the Right Call

An interactive workplace simulation that helps employees use AI tools responsibly, protect sensitive information, verify AI-generated content, and make better decisions during real-world tasks.

Launch Interactive Experience Storyboard Design Document AI Job Aid

Business Problem

Employees are increasingly using generative AI tools at work, but many do not know when AI is appropriate, what information should never be shared, or how to verify AI-generated output before using it.

Target Audience

Office employees, customer-service teams, project staff, and new hires who use AI tools for writing, research, summarization, planning, and everyday workplace tasks.

Learning Objectives

  • Recognize when AI is appropriate for a workplace task.
  • Identify sensitive information that should not be entered into public AI tools.
  • Write clearer prompts that produce more useful AI responses.
  • Verify AI-generated information before using or sharing it.
  • Choose appropriate actions when AI output is inaccurate, incomplete, or risky.

Interactive Experience

The learner follows a realistic workday and encounters a series of video-based decisions. Each decision changes the scenario and provides immediate consequence-based feedback.

  • Interactive video introduction
  • Branching workplace decisions
  • Immediate feedback
  • AI prompt challenge
  • Privacy and data-security decision
  • Final scenario assessment
  • Performance score and completion result

Scenario Example

Your manager asks you to summarize a client report using AI before a 3:00 PM meeting. The report contains client names, contact information, and confidential business details.

What should you do?

The learner chooses from multiple options and sees the consequence of the decision before continuing the simulation.

A quick-reference guide employees can use after training.

Evaluation Plan

Learner performance is measured through scenario decisions, accuracy of final challenge responses, successful identification of privacy risks, and the ability to evaluate AI-generated content before use.

Design Rationale

Instead of presenting AI rules as a traditional information-heavy course, this experience places learners inside realistic workplace situations. Learners make decisions, experience consequences, and practice applying safe and effective AI behaviors.
