# New Hire Launchpad — Design Document

## 1. Performance Problem
New employees experience cognitive overload and uncertainty about priorities, communication, and early expectations.

## 2. Target Audience
New corporate employees during their first 30 days.

## 3. Instructional Goal
Enable learners to perform the target behaviors confidently in realistic workplace situations.

## 4. Learning Objectives
- Prioritize the three actions that matter most during the first week.
- Select the appropriate communication channel for common workplace needs.
- Use the 30-day roadmap to prepare for manager check-ins and early deliverables.

## 5. Instructional Strategy
- Use short content segments followed by application.
- Favor realistic decisions over recall-only questions.
- Give immediate, explanatory feedback.
- Keep navigation simple and accessible.
- Provide a job aid for use after training.

## 6. Course Outline
1. Welcome and role clarity
2. Day 1 essentials
3. First-week priorities
4. Communication choices
5. 30-day roadmap
6. Knowledge check

## 7. Assessment Strategy
Scenario-based checks after each content chunk and a final five-item knowledge check.

## 8. Evaluation Plan
L1: onboarding confidence; L2: knowledge check; L3: manager rating of role readiness and reduction in repeated first-week questions.

## 9. Accessibility Considerations
- Keyboard-accessible buttons and links.
- High contrast text and simple layouts.
- Meaning is never communicated by color alone.
- Plain-language instructions.
- Responsive layout for smaller screens.

## 10. Prototype Notes
This portfolio version is a functional HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype intended to demonstrate the instructional strategy and interaction design.
