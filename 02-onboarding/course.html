02 — New Hire Launchpad Case Study

INTERACTIVE LEARNING PROTOTYPE

Your First 30 Days

Practice realistic decisions a new employee may face during the first few weeks on the job.

Scenario 1 — Your First Morning

You receive several emails, meeting invitations, system-access requests, and onboarding tasks at the same time. What should you focus on first?

Start with required system access, your manager's instructions, and the highest-priority onboarding tasks before handling lower-priority requests.

Scenario 2 — Information Overload

You have received policies, links, training materials, and messages from several departments. What is the most effective approach?

Organize information by priority, complete required items first, and use the onboarding roadmap or checklist to avoid missing important steps.

Scenario 3 — Communication Choice

You need a quick answer about an urgent task. Which communication approach should you use?

Use the communication channel your team has identified for urgent questions, and contact the correct person rather than sending the same request through multiple channels.

Scenario 4 — End of Week One

Before finishing your first week, what should you confirm?

Confirm your system access, required training, key contacts, upcoming meetings, priorities, and expectations with your manager.

Learning Goal

By completing this experience, learners practice prioritizing onboarding tasks, organizing information, choosing appropriate communication channels, and preparing for manager check-ins.
