First 30 Days Roadmap

Use this quick reference at the moment of need.

1

Day 1

Confirm access, meet your manager, understand your role, and identify where essential resources live.

2

Week 1

Meet key partners, clarify priorities, complete required training, and surface blockers early.

3

Weeks 2–3

Begin core deliverables, ask for feedback, and document questions or recurring process gaps.

4

Day 30

Review progress with your manager, confirm next priorities, and identify one development goal.

Remember: When risk or authority is unclear, pause and use the approved escalation path.
