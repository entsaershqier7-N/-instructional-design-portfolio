Case Study

New Hire Launchpad

A structured onboarding journey that turns an overwhelming first week into a clear sequence of actions.

Launch Interactive CourseStoryboardDesign DocumentJob Aid
Business problem

New hires receive policies, links, meetings, and tools all at once. This creates cognitive overload and repeated support questions while delaying productive work.

Audience

New corporate employees in their first 30 days, including hybrid and remote staff.

Solution

['Prioritize the three actions that matter most during the first week.', 'Select the appropriate communication channel for common workplace needs.', 'Use the 30-day roadmap to prepare for manager check-ins and early deliverables.']

Deliverables

Journey Map

Maps learner needs from preboarding through Day 30.

First-week priorities, communication decisions, and practice.

Manager Checklist

Performance support for structured check-ins.

Evaluation Plan

Measures confidence, knowledge, and early role readiness.

Knowledge-check target.

Design rationale

The solution emphasizes decisions and job behaviors rather than information exposure. Practice is aligned directly to the objectives, and feedback explains consequences so learners can build judgment.
