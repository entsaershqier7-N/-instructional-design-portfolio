Business Problem

New hires receive policies, links, meetings, and tools all at once. This experience organizes critical information into a clear, action-oriented learning journey.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the most important actions during the first weeks of employment.
  • Locate essential workplace resources and support channels.
  • Apply onboarding information to realistic workplace situations.

Evaluation Plan

Learner performance is evaluated through scenario-based decisions, knowledge checks, and successful completion of onboarding activities.

Design Rationale

The solution focuses on practical decisions and job behaviors rather than information overload, helping employees become productive faster.
