# Service Recovery Lab — Coaching Guide

## Debrief prompts
1. What did the customer need emotionally before they were ready to hear a solution?
2. Which facts had to be verified before offering a remedy?
3. What promise would be risky to make too early?
4. When does escalation add value, and when does it simply transfer the problem?
5. How would you phrase the next step so the customer knows exactly what will happen?

## Observation rubric
- Acknowledges emotion without arguing.
- Asks focused clarification questions.
- Chooses a resolution within authority.
- Sets a clear expectation for timing.
- Escalates only when criteria are met.
