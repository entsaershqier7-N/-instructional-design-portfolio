Business Problem

Customer service employees must resolve customer concerns while balancing empathy, company policy, and appropriate escalation. Inconsistent responses can lead to poor customer experiences and unnecessary escalations.

Learning Objectives

  • Use empathy and active listening when responding to frustrated customers.
  • Identify the appropriate response to common customer-service situations.
  • Apply service-recovery techniques to resolve customer concerns.
  • Recognize when an issue should be escalated.

Evaluation Plan

Learner performance is evaluated through scenario-based decisions, immediate feedback, and successful completion of realistic customer-service situations.

Design Rationale

The experience focuses on realistic workplace decisions rather than information-heavy training. Learners practice handling difficult customer situations in a safe environment and receive immediate feedback.
