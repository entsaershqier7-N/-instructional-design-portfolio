Business Problem

Customer-service employees may encounter frustrated customers while balancing company policies, customer expectations, and escalation procedures. This learning experience provides realistic practice before employees face these situations on the job.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply active-listening techniques when responding to frustrated customers.
  • Select an appropriate response to common customer-service situations.
  • Choose when to resolve an issue independently and when to escalate it.

Evaluation Plan

Learner performance is evaluated through scenario-based decisions, immediate feedback, and successful completion of realistic customer-service situations.

Design Rationale

The solution focuses on realistic workplace decisions rather than information-heavy training. Learners practice handling difficult customer situations in a safe environment and receive immediate feedback.
