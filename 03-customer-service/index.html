Service Recovery Lab

A branching customer-service practice experience focused on empathy, resolution, and escalation.

Launch Interactive CourseStoryboardDesign DocumentJob Aid
Business problem

Representatives may know policy but struggle to apply it while a customer is frustrated. Script memorization alone does not build judgment.

Audience

Customer-service representatives who handle phone, chat, or digital support interactions.

Solution

["Use an empathy statement that acknowledges the customer's experience without admitting unsupported fault.", "Select a resolution that matches the issue and the representative's authority.", 'Recognize when a situation requires escalation.']

Deliverables

Branching Scenario

Learners make decisions across a realistic service-recovery conversation.

Feedback Model

Every decision explains the likely customer and business consequence.

Job Aid

Acknowledge–Clarify–Resolve–Confirm–Escalate quick reference.

Coaching Guide

Debrief prompts for manager-led practice.

Evaluation plan

80%

Scenario proficiency target.

↓ Escalations

Reduce avoidable escalations.

↑ CSAT

Track service recovery satisfaction.

Design rationale

The solution emphasizes decisions and job behaviors rather than information exposure. Practice is aligned directly to the objectives, and feedback explains consequences so learners can build judgment.
