# Service Recovery Lab — Design Document

## 1. Performance Problem
Representatives understand service policies but need practice applying them in emotional and ambiguous conversations.

## 2. Target Audience
Customer-service representatives in phone, chat, and digital support environments.

## 3. Instructional Goal
Enable learners to perform the target behaviors confidently in realistic workplace situations.

## 4. Learning Objectives
- Use an empathy statement that acknowledges the customer's experience without admitting unsupported fault.
- Select a resolution that matches the issue and the representative's authority.
- Recognize when a situation requires escalation.

## 5. Instructional Strategy
- Use short content segments followed by application.
- Favor realistic decisions over recall-only questions.
- Give immediate, explanatory feedback.
- Keep navigation simple and accessible.
- Provide a job aid for use after training.

## 6. Course Outline
1. Why recovery conversations fail
2. Acknowledge without arguing
3. Clarify the actual problem
4. Choose a resolution
5. Escalation criteria
6. Branching practice

## 7. Assessment Strategy
Branching decisions scored on empathy, information gathering, resolution fit, and escalation judgment.

## 8. Evaluation Plan
L1: relevance/confidence; L2: scenario score; L3: quality-monitoring rubric; L4: CSAT and avoidable escalation trends.

## 9. Accessibility Considerations
- Keyboard-accessible buttons and links.
- High contrast text and simple layouts.
- Meaning is never communicated by color alone.
- Plain-language instructions.
- Responsive layout for smaller screens.

## 10. Prototype Notes
This portfolio version is a functional HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype intended to demonstrate the instructional strategy and interaction design.
