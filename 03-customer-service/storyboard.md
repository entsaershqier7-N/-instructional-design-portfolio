# Service Recovery Lab — Storyboard

## Scenario
Customer: “I've contacted support twice and nobody fixed this. I was charged again.”

### Decision 1 — Opening response
A. “You need to calm down.” → Defensive; increases friction.  
B. “I can see why this is frustrating. Let me confirm what happened so I can help.” → Best.  
C. “That was another agent.” → Deflects responsibility.

### Decision 2 — Clarification
Best practice: verify the duplicate charge, date, amount, and prior case notes before promising a remedy.

### Decision 3 — Resolution
Choose the remedy that is within authority and explain the next step and timing.

### Decision 4 — Escalation
Escalate when the issue exceeds authority, creates legal/safety risk, or cannot be resolved using the approved path.

## Feedback Philosophy
Feedback explains consequences rather than simply displaying “correct/incorrect.”
