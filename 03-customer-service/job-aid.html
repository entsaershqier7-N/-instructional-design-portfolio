Service Recovery: A-C-R-C-E

Use this quick reference at the moment of need.

1

Acknowledge

Name the customer's frustration or inconvenience in neutral, respectful language.

2

Clarify

Confirm the issue, desired outcome, relevant facts, and previous actions.

3

Resolve

Offer the best approved action within your authority and explain why it fits.

4

Confirm

State what will happen next, who owns it, and the expected timing.

5

Escalate

Escalate when the issue exceeds authority or meets a defined risk/exception criterion.

Remember: When risk or authority is unclear, pause and use the approved escalation path.
